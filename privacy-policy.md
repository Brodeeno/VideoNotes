# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 21 August 2026

Thank you for using this extension. Your privacy matters, and this extension was built following a **Privacy First** approach.

## Data Collection

This extension does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.

## Local Storage

All notes you create for Instagram and YouTube posts/videos are stored **exclusively on your device**, using the browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API. This data:

- Never leaves your browser
- Is never transmitted to the developer or any third party
- Is not accessible by anyone other than you, on your own device

If you uninstall the extension, this locally stored data may be removed by the browser as well.

## Host Permissions

This extension requests access to **instagram.com** and **youtube.com** solely to:

- Detect individual posts and videos on these pages
- Display the note-taking interface directly within them

No content, browsing activity, or personal data from these websites is collected, read beyond what's needed for this function, or shared with third parties.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not integrate with, or send data to, any third-party service, API, or analytics provider.

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age, since no data is collected at all.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in the extension's functionality. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
